Note: This codebook and the corresponding data files are complete, with one caveat.  Although I have asked Luskin about this issue, I have not heard a response from him.  There is something weird about the breast cancer question.  The question is a true/false question:  Every women in Britain gets free breast cancer screenings (true/false).  However, according to the information, all women between certain ages get free screenings.  I'm not sure whether the correct answer is true or false with the given question wording.  Certainly, technically it is false, but I think the answer they were anticipating when they wrote the question is "true."
