Note: The general design of this survey is as follows.  Individuals were asked 
several question in person.  These are the "A" questions such as QA1, QA2a, 
QA2b, etc.  They were then handed a self-completion booklet which was filled 
out in the presence of the interviewer.  These are time 1 (T1) variables.  
After completing the time 1 questionnaire, respondents were asked some 
additional questions by the interviewer.  These are the "B" face-to-face 
interview questions such as QB1a, QB1b, etc.  All respondents were invited to 
attend the deliberative opinion poll in Manchester.  For those who came, after 
the DOP, they were given another self-completion questionnaire.  These are the 
time 2 (T2) variables.  Those variables without a question number are either 
administrative variables or summary variables.

NOTE about question QA8a:  (NOTE: To get individuals accustomed to the 
question format that was later used in the self-completion part of the 
process, individuals were asked a sample question with the same format.  This 
allowed the individual to get accustomed to a format which might otherwise be 
new to them.  It also allowed to interviewer to guide the respondent though 
the question.  The specific question asked was chosen for its simplicity and 
the responses are of no substantive use to the project.  Therefore, the data 
which correspond with question A8a and QA8b are not available.)
	"(Interviewer: Hand over folded 'Scales Sheet' with side A facing up)  
Please look at this sheet.  Some people are convinced that government should 
definitely privatise the Post Office.  They would tick the top box (Point to 
Top Box).  Other people are convinced that government should definitely not 
privatise the Post Office.  They would tick the bottom box (Point to Bottom 
Box).  And others are not so sure, and would pick one of the boxes somewhere 
between (Point to Boxes In Between).
	"Firstly, please tick which of the seven boxes comes closes to your own 
views about privatising the Post Office.
	"Now turn the sheet over and tick which of the seven boxes you think 
comes closest to the views of the Conservative Party about privatising the 
Post Office.  And then which of the seven boxes you think comes closest to the 
views of the Labour Party."
