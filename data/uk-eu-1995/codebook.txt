Codebook for British Deliberative Opinion Poll on the Future of Britain in Europe

This codebook is located at:
	/home/gov/staff/poll96/europe/codebook/europe.code

The data for this codebook are located at:
	/home/gov/staff/poll96/europe/europe_data/
There are two versions of the data, one without answers such as "don't know" or "can't choose" coded as missing.  In other words, all information is retained.  These data are europe.por (SPSS version) and europe.ssd01 (SAS version).  The other version codes as missing responses not expressing opinions.  These data are europe_2.por (SPSS version) and europe_2.ssd01 (SAS version).


Note about the survey methodology:  Who conducted the survey.  Dates in field.  Date of DOP.  Number of respondents in original sample.  Number of T1 respondents, etc.  
	The time one survey methodology was a door-to-door interview with a self administered questionnaire (SAQ1) section sandwiched between two sets of interviewer administered questions (IAQ).  For the IAQ questionnaire, "A" questions (such as A1) are asked before the SAQ1 is administered and the "B" questions (such as B3) are asked after the SAQ1.  At time 2, all questionnaires were self-administered (SAQ2).
